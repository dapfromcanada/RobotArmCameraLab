# Bench findings 2026-10-21 (Derek + camera_lab)

Cameras: Lucid Triton TRI054S-C (233100505), JAI AP-3200T-PGE (U330439). Charts: Letter
step wedge, grey patch, resolution, tag card. Raw tables: the other files in this folder.

## User sets
- **JAI:** `UserSetSave` is refused while acquiring ("Node is not writable") and works with
  the stream stopped. Proven: save → change exposure/WB → `UserSetLoad` → saved values back.
  User Set 1 now holds the setup values (14 959 µs, 0 dB, WB R 0.973 / B 1.176). The JAI has
  no `UserSetDefault`: whether it **boots** from User Set 1 needs a power-cycle test.
- **TRI054S:** save to User Set 1 + `UserSetDefault = UserSet1` worked. Its user sets do NOT
  store `HDRTuningEnable` or the HDR channel exposures/gains, so HDR on/off and the channel
  values are not restored by a power cycle.

## Brightness and noise
- Exposure × gain is linear on both cameras (constant output across the sweeps).
- Exposure longer than one frame lowers the frame rate (TRI054S: 61 ms → 16 fps,
  122 ms → 8 fps, 244 ms → 4 fps).
- **Spatial σ on a printed patch is not camera noise.** It measures print texture and the
  light gradient (it did not change with gain or with frame averaging). Use **temporal
  noise** (two frames differenced, /√2) for gain limits.
- Temporal SNR at equal brightness (12-bit, centre crop, median of 3 frame pairs):

  | gain dB | 0 | 6 | 12 | 18 | 24 | 30 | 36 | 42 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | TRI054S SNR (level 0.115) | 142 | 93 | 74 | **108** | 86 | 76 | 67 | 52 |
  | JAI SNR (level 0.178) | 50 | 36 | 25 | 18 | | | | |

  JAI: ×1.4 SNR loss per +6 dB (shot-noise rule). TRI054S: 18 dB is quieter than 12 dB,
  consistent with a sensor gain-mode switch (e.g. dual conversion gain) — verify in the
  fingerprint before relying on it.
- **Frame averaging works:** 4 frames halved the temporal noise on both cameras (√4).
- No light flicker in the bench light (frame-to-frame brightness change 0.01–0.04 %,
  no rolling-shutter banding).
- 8-bit hides the TRI054S's noise floor (σ below one 8-bit step at low gain): fingerprints
  must use 12-bit formats.

## HDR (TRI054S, gains fixed 19.8 / 4.2 / 29.4 / 4.2 dB)
- Wedge white vs channel exposure: 5 ms 0.34, 10 ms 0.48, 20 ms 0.68, 30 ms 0.83,
  40 ms 0.96, 47.9 ms 1.00 (clipped). Doubling exposure gives ×1.4: tone-compressed output.
- The printed step wedge resolves ~15 steps with HDR on or off: the print's own range is
  too small to show HDR's benefit. That needs a high-dynamic-range scene (bright highlights
  and dark shadows in one view).

- The setup now enters the profile's mode first (the TRI054S setup had used HDR knobs while
  the camera was in HDR-off).
- TRI054S HDR: channel exposure capped at one frame (47.9 ms), gains fixed (profile).
- TRI054S explicit frame-rate changes pause the stream (the GUI slider was refused).

- BlackLevel is % of the 12-bit full scale on a channel output (1 → ~40 DN, 5 → ~205 DN).

- Channel 0 is saturated at 100 µs on the lit acrylic: it needs far less light for its
  photon transfer (the fingerprint's `channels` step asks for it).

## 2026-10-23: TRI054S-C raw_hdr (BayerRG24) — quantization step and SNR vs level

Opal acrylic on the lens, dimmer 6, iris f/1.4, exposure swept 86 µs – 105 ms; centre green
plane; σ = temporal (two frames differenced / √2); step = median gap between distinct values.

| level % FS | step DN | σ DN | SNR |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.08 | 26 | 2 156 | 6 |
| 0.50 | 26 | 3 378 | 25 |
| 0.99 | 577 | 5 315 | 31 |
| 4.1 | 577 | 10 358 | 67 |
| 8.2 | 577 | 14 362 | **96** |
| 9.2 | mixed | 24 308 | 63 |
| 10.0 | mixed | 62 360 | 27 |
| 10.8 | mixed | 86 093 | **21** |
| 11.7 | 4 157 | 88 724 | 22 |
| 19.7 | 4 157 | 108 166 | 31 |
| 29.5 | 4 157 | 128 497 | 38 |
| 44.1 | 4 157 | 155 904 | 47 |
| 58.8 | 4 157 | 178 788 | 55 |
| 73.5 | 4 157 | 200 208 | 62 |
| 99.5 | 4 159 | (saturating) | |

- Not a ×64-scaled 18-bit signal: values are odd and even alike; the "18.0 stops" was a
  coincidence. The step grows in jumps as the on-chip merge hands over to less sensitive
  channels: ≈ 19.3 effective bits at the bottom (26 DN), 14.8 from ≈ 1 % (577 DN), 12.0 from
  ≈ 10 % (4 157 DN). Below ≈ 0.01 % even finer, irregular codes (dark run: 0, 13, 14, 21, …).
  The step is ≤ 1/8 of the noise everywhere: quantization never limits the data.
- **SNR is not monotonic:** peak 96 just below 9 % FS, a 4× dip to ≈ 21 at the ≈ 10 %
  handover, back to ≈ 60 at 75 %. The profile's raw_hdr target mean 0.10 sits in the dip.
- Step ratios: 577 / 26 ≈ 22 (ch0 vs ch1 sensitivity, earlier estimated 6–12×),
  4 157 / 577 ≈ 7.2.
- Light 3.6× lower than the 2026-10-22 dimmer sweep at the same dimmer setting (10 ms: 10 %
  vs 36 %): lights, acrylic position or iris position differ — to check.

## 2026-10-23: light flicker and row noise (TRI054S-C raw_hdr, acrylic on the lens)

Row banding = std of the row means of each frame's temporal deviation, vs what pixel noise
alone gives; 16 frames per exposure, centre of the green plane.

- **Dimmed LED panels (dimmer 6):** bands 1.8–2.8 % of the signal at 0.5–4 ms (≈ 20× noise),
  0.13 % at 8 ms; period 177 plane rows = 354 sensor rows ≈ 8.3 ms → **120 Hz** (60 Hz mains,
  full-wave). Frame-mean changes 0.2–0.3 % (≈ 40× noise) at short exposures. Explains the
  short-exposure noise differences between sweeps and contaminates the 0.1–3 ms HDR channel
  measurements of 2026-10-22.
- **DC LED floods, full, iris f/1.4:** no 120 Hz; a **≈ 4 kHz** ripple (period 5 plane rows
  ≈ 10 sensor rows ≈ 0.24 ms): bands 8.4 % at 0.1 ms, 1.5 % at 0.25 ms, 0.6–0.9 % at
  0.5–1 ms, ≈ 0.25 % at 2–4 ms, near noise from 8 ms.
- **Lens cap on:** row noise ≈ 8 DN rms at every exposure (0.1–12 ms), ≈ 4.3× the
  uncorrelated expectation but only ≈ 1/8 of the 60–64 DN pixel noise; broad, no 5-row
  period → the 4 kHz pattern is the floods', not the sensor's. Black 194–197 DN over 0.1–12 ms
  (dark current ≈ 0).
- Rolling-shutter row time from the 120 Hz bands: 8.33 ms / 354 rows ≈ **23.5 µs per row**.
- Consequences for the fingerprint: lit measurements now use ≥ 2 ms (bench
  `min_exposure_us`), and every sweep point's row-correlated variance is checked: banding
  that adds > 2 % to the noise (beyond the dark row noise) stops the step.
