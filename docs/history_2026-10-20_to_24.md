The fingerprint is now HDR-aware (8 steps): it measures in each camera's design mode (the profile default) and that mode's ≥ 12-bit format; raises BlackLevel to 5 % where offered (restored at the end); for HDR cameras the combined output gets a response check (linearity, saturation, SNR across the range; no photon transfer, EMVA 1288 applies per channel), the darks include each channel's black/read noise, and a new `channels` step fits photon transfer per HDR channel on its 12-bit output, each at its own light level (re-run with more/less light; measured channels are kept); the gain steps are skipped where gain is not a knob of the design mode.
